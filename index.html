About Flip Duel

Flip Duel is a fast parkour flipping game built around timing, skill combos, tight landing zones, and ragdoll crashes. Play it in your browser, stick the landing, or watch the wipeout when the timing is off.

Start in 1 Player campaign, challenge a friend in local 2 Player, or stay in FreeStyle until the life bar runs out. There are 8 levels, 80 parkour challenges, unlockable flip skills, and six characters.

How to Play Flip Duel

The game loads in the frame above. Click the game so keyboard and mouse input go to Flip Duel. Charge the jump, pick a flip skill, then land feet-first on the platform.

Core controls

  • Mouse: control the character and perform flips
  • WASD: character movement
  • Skill panel: add, remove, or switch flip moves
  • Same PC 2 Players: Player 1 uses WASD, Player 2 uses Arrow keys
  • Mobile: tap the screen and use on-screen skill buttons

Game modes

  • 1 Player: full parkour campaign from The Field through 80 stages.
  • 2 Player: local duel on the same keyboard.
  • FreeStyle: keep clearing jumps until the health bar is empty.

Tips

  • Choose the skill before you jump. Rotation speed has to match the landing zone.
  • Release just before your feet hit the platform so you stay upright.
  • Narrow platforms punish extra spin. Drop a fast trick if the pad is small.
  • Mix Pike, Scissor, Knee Tuck, Layout, Viper, and Super Flip as they unlock.
