What is Geometry Vibes 3D?

Geometry Vibes 3D is the official site for the 3D version of the game, made by gameVgames. The first Geometry Vibes is a flat wave. This one rebuilds the stages in 3D, with a new camera, new art, and systems the original never had.

Classic Mode is split into Rookie, Pro, and Expert. Each Classic level holds three stars. Across those difficulties you can pick up 90 stars in total (30 per difficulty). Stars are both a completion mark and the currency for the Market, where you buy arrow skins and 3D trails.

Endless Mode has a global leaderboard. Local play is 2 Player only, not four, so the 3D view stays readable. The browser game is on this page. The iOS and Android apps use the store buttons on Play and in the footer.

How to play on this site

Press Play, then click the game so input hits the canvas. Jump through the 3D path, grab stars in Classic, or push Endless distance for the board.

Controls

  • Player 1: W, left mouse click, or Space to jump
  • Player 2: Up Arrow
  • Phone and tablet: tap anywhere to jump

Modes

  • Classic: 3D levels, three stars each, Rookie / Pro / Expert.
  • Endless: distance run with a worldwide leaderboard.
  • 2 Player: one keyboard, two people. Player 2 is Up Arrow.

Other games in the series

The original Geometry Vibes, plus X-Ball, X-Arrow, and Monster, are in the row above. Those links go to gameVgames.

Tips

  • Learn the 3D camera on Rookie before Pro and Expert.
  • Replay Classic stages for missing stars. The Market needs them.
  • Jump with the beat. Depth makes late taps clip walls that look open.
  • Two players share one screen. Keep the layout simple; that is why 4-player did not carry over.

FAQ

Is Geometry Vibes 3D free?

Yes. Play in the browser here with no account, or install the iOS and Android apps.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes, locally. 2 Player Mode is on one computer. There is no online versus; Endless still ranks you on the global board.

Does Geometry Vibes 3D work on mobile?

Yes. Tap to jump in the browser, or use the Geometry Vibes 3D apps on iPhone and Android.
